How FileMate AI Works

Upload a file, ask for a task, and work with your material in one place.

📤

1. Upload & Organize

Upload PDFs, PowerPoint presentations, text documents, images and other supported files. FileMate suggests a clearer title using the file name and file type.

🎙️

2. Speak Your Request

Ask the voice assistant to open a named file, make a quiz, create a practice sheet, or show your file library.

🧠

3. Study & Practice

Use extracted text from supported files to create a practice quiz or revision sheet, then review your answers in the Study Workspace.
